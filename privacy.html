Agent John Smith

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

Agent John Smith (“the Service”) is operated by Ryan Zell (“we”, “us”). The Service is a personal SMS assistant that sends reminders, Home Assistant alerts, summaries, system status notifications, and responses to user commands via text message to (301) 900-3525. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

  • Phone numbers — the mobile number you use to opt in, and any phone numbers you include in messages or that appear in connected personal data (for example contacts) when you ask the assistant to use them.
  • SMS content — messages you send to the Service and messages the Service sends to you.
  • Assistant context — information needed to fulfill your requests, such as reminders, calendar details, smart-home alerts, system status, and related personal data you authorize the assistant to access.
  • Technical delivery data — message timestamps, delivery status, and similar logs required to operate SMS delivery through our messaging provider (Twilio).

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide and operate the SMS personal assistant Service
  • Honor opt-in, help, and opt-out requests
  • Send the notifications and responses you request or enable
  • Maintain security, reliability, and support for the Service

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use your SMS content for third-party advertising. No mobile information or SMS opt-in data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the Service, including with our SMS infrastructure provider (Twilio) for message delivery, and when required by law. Mobile numbers collected for SMS enrollment are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Personal assistant integrations you connect (for example Home Assistant or calendar sources) may process data according to those systems’ own policies.

Messaging consent

You opt in by texting START to (301) 900-3525 and replying Y to the confirmation request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Opting out stops further Service messages to that number.

Retention

We retain message and account-related data only as long as needed to operate the Service, honor legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your SMS enrollment data by contacting support.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information processed by the Service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Contact

Ryan Zell, operator of Agent John Smith. For privacy questions or requests, visit https://agentjohnsmith.github.io/ or text HELP to (301) 900-3525.
